BookAlong / Privacy隐私政策

Your books stay yours.你的书，留在自己的阅读空间。

Updated / effective: 5 November 2026更新／生效日期：2026 年 11 月 5 日

About this policy关于本政策

BookAlong is independently developed and published under the BookAlong name by Johnny Xu. This policy covers the Apple TV app, the Mac Books companion and this website. For support and privacy requests: xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com.

BookAlong（随阅）是 Johnny Xu 独立开发的产品。本政策适用于 Apple TV 应用、Mac Books 同步工具及本网站。支持与隐私联系：xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com。

Local reading data本地阅读数据

Apple TV stores imported or downloaded book copies, covers, library metadata, appearance preferences, reading positions and bookmarks. Book layout and supported text search run on the device. The app has no developer-operated account system.

Apple TV 保存导入或下载的原书副本、封面、书目、外观偏好、阅读位置与书签。排版及支持的文字搜索在设备上完成。应用没有开发者运营的账号系统。

Mac access to Apple BooksMac 读取 Apple Books

When you enable the Mac companion, it reads the authorized local Apple Books library metadata, file locations, supported book files and All Books sorting information. It records source mappings and sync status in its own application data. It does not read Apple Books annotation databases or reading positions, and does not write back to Books or modify its originals. It waits for Books to download unavailable source files.

开启 Mac 同步工具后，它会读取你授权的本机 Apple Books 书目、文件位置、支持的原书文件及「全部书籍」排序信息，在自己的应用数据中保存来源映射与同步状态。它不读取 Books 批注数据库或阅读位置，不回写 Books，也不修改来源原书。来源尚未下载时会等待 Books 下载。

Permissions and background activity权限与后台运行

Books access may require macOS permission to read other apps’ data, or Full Disk Access for this installed app. Full Disk Access is a broad system permission; the companion uses it to read the Books library and referenced files. No Accessibility, Screen Recording or Books automation permission is required. You choose whether to enable sync and launch at login. You can pause or quit from the menu bar and revoke permissions in System Settings.

读取 Books 可能需要 macOS 的其他应用数据访问授权，或为这个已安装的应用开启完全磁盘访问权限。完全磁盘访问是系统较广权限；工具实际用于读取 Books 书库及其引用的文件。不需要辅助功能、屏幕录制或自动化控制 Books 的权限。同步及登录时启动由你主动开启，可从菜单栏暂停或退出，也可在系统设置撤销权限。

Private iCloud library私有 iCloud 书库

Cloud-enabled builds use Apple CloudKit’s private database for your iCloud Apple Account. Supported book files, covers, titles, authors, source identifiers and book order are uploaded for the library to be available on your devices. BookAlong’s sync protocol also stores its own library records, reading positions, bookmarks, collections, memberships and deletion markers; existing BookAlong state is retained. This does not sync Apple Books reading positions. The same iCloud account, container and environment are needed on both devices. The service uses your iCloud storage and is provided by Apple.

启用云功能的构建使用你 iCloud Apple 账户对应的 Apple CloudKit 私有数据库。支持的原书、封面、书名、作者、来源标识与书籍顺序会上传，供自己的其他设备使用。随阅同步协议也保存自身的书目、阅读位置、书签、集合、成员关系与删除标记；已有随阅状态予以保留。这不包含 Apple Books 阅读位置同步。两端需使用同一 iCloud 账号、容器与环境。该服务由 Apple 提供，并使用你的 iCloud 存储空间。

Local network transfer局域网文件传输

Opening Upload on Apple TV starts a temporary local HTTP receiver for books and optional reading-state backups. It requires no pairing code: other devices on the same network can use it while it is open. Use a trusted network. Receiving stops when you manually go back or stop it, the app enters the background, or the ten-minute session expires. Successful batch uploads may return to the library while receiving continues. Files are not routed through this website or a developer server.

在 Apple TV 打开「上传」会启动临时本地 HTTP 接收器，用于书籍及可选阅读状态备份。无需配对码，开启时同一网络的其他设备可以使用，请仅在可信网络使用。手动返回或停止、应用进入后台，或十分钟会话到期会关闭接收。批量上传成功自动回到书库时，接收仍可能继续。文件不经本网站或开发者服务器中转。

Removal, retention and backups移除、保留与备份

tvOS can clear cached book files and covers. Keep originals outside the app. Local book removal deletes the local entry and its associated reading data. A cloud download can be removed without removing its cloud library entry. Source removals in Books, or the selected overflow-removal policy, can remove companion-created mirrored library entries and their uploaded book assets after confirmation; failed or incomplete source scans do not infer deletion. Sync records and deletion markers may remain to prevent removed entries from returning. Pausing sync does not erase existing iCloud uploads. Reading-state exports contain metadata, settings, positions and bookmarks, not original book files.

tvOS 可能清理原书与封面缓存，请在应用外保留原文件。移除本地书籍会删除本地书目及相关阅读状态。云书籍可仅移除下载，保留云书目。从 Books 移除来源，或选择超范围移除策略后，可能在确认后移除工具创建的镜像书目及上传的原书资产；扫描失败或不完整时不推断删除。同步记录和删除标记可能继续保留，以防已删除书目重新出现。暂停同步不会擦除既有 iCloud 上传。阅读状态导出包含书目、设置、位置与书签，不含原书。

Home Screen artwork主屏幕书架

The Top Shelf image is fixed bundled artwork. It does not display private book covers, titles or reading positions. A local App Group shares the display switch with the bundled extension.

Top Shelf 书架为内置固定图片，不显示私人封面、书名或阅读位置。本地 App Group 仅向自身扩展共享显示开关。

Advertising and analytics广告与分析

BookAlong has no ads, third-party analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking. We do not operate a server that receives your book library or reading history. The website uses local storage to remember your chosen language and has no analytics or contact-form backend.

随阅没有广告、第三方分析 SDK 或跨应用追踪。我们不运营接收你书库或阅读历史的服务器。本网站使用浏览器本地存储记住语言选择，没有分析脚本或联系表单后台。

Website and support correspondence网站与支持邮件

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process ordinary connection information, including IP addresses, under its own policies. Sending support email lets your mail provider and ours process your message. We use correspondence to respond to you and retain it only as reasonably needed for support and applicable legal requirements. Request deletion at the support address. Do not send private books, passwords or account credentials.

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，托管方可能按其政策处理 IP 地址等普通连接信息。发送支持邮件时，双方邮箱服务商会处理邮件。我们仅用来回复并在合理必要的支持期间及适用法律要求下保留。可通过支持邮箱请求删除。请勿发送私人书籍、密码或账号凭据。

Your choices and updates你的选择与政策更新

Local reading does not require iCloud or the Mac companion. You can pause sync, stop local receiving, disable login launch, revoke Books permissions and manage local files and backups. There is no BookAlong developer account to delete. Cloud data remains associated with your Apple Account under Apple’s services; uninstalling the app does not by itself erase iCloud data. Contact support with questions about retained sync records. We update this policy when the product or its data handling changes.

本地阅读无需 iCloud 或 Mac 工具。你可暂停同步、停止本地接收、关闭登录启动、撤销 Books 权限，以及管理本地文件与备份。没有需要注销的随阅开发者账号。云数据仍关联 Apple 服务下的你的 Apple 账户，卸载应用本身不等于擦除 iCloud 数据。对保留同步记录有疑问可联系支持。产品或数据处理变化时会更新本政策。
