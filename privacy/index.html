Your books stay yours.

你的书，留在你的阅读空间。

Effective / updated: 生效／更新日期：2026-10-25

About this policy关于本政策

TVBooks is published by Johnny Xu. This policy describes the first public television release and its support website. Contact: xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com.

TVBooks 由 Johnny Xu 发布。本政策说明首发电视应用和支持网站的数据处理方式。联系：xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com。

Your books and reading data书籍与阅读数据

The app stores your chosen EPUB files, library information, appearance settings, reading positions and bookmarks on your Apple TV. EPUB layout and in-book search are performed on the device. We do not operate a server that receives your library or reading history.

应用在 Apple TV 保存你选择的 EPUB、书库信息、外观设置、阅读位置和书签。EPUB 排版及书内搜索在设备上完成。我们不运营接收你书库或阅读历史的服务器。

Local file transfer局域网文件传输

When you open the upload screen, a temporary receiver accepts files and optional reading-state backups from a device you choose on the same network. A temporary pairing code is required. Receiving stops when you leave, stop it or the session expires. This transfer uses local HTTP, so use a network you trust. Files are not routed through our website or a developer-operated cloud service.

打开上传页面时，应用临时接收同网络内你选择的设备发送的文件及可选阅读状态备份，并要求临时配对码。离开、停止或会话到期即关闭接收。局域网传输使用 HTTP，请在可信网络使用。文件不经本网站或开发者云服务中转。

Home Screen artwork主屏幕书架图片

The Home Screen bookshelf is a fixed image included with the app. It does not use your private covers, book titles or reading progress. The app shares only its display switch with its bundled Top Shelf extension through a local App Group. Earlier development preview snapshots and generated private cover walls are removed when the updated app opens.

主屏幕书架为应用内置的固定图片，不使用私人封面、书名或阅读进度。应用通过本地 App Group 仅向自身 Top Shelf 扩展共享显示开关。升级后打开应用会清理此前开发预览版共享的私人书目和墙图。

Cloud services and source files云服务与来源文件

The first public release does not upload books to a TVBooks CloudKit library or synchronize Apple Books progress. You may select files you have already downloaded from iCloud Drive using your browser or operating system. That source service is provided by Apple under its own terms. The optional Mac importer reads user-chosen downloaded files without changing the originals and is not required for the television app.

首发公开版本不会向 TVBooks CloudKit 书库上传书籍，也不与 Apple Books 同步进度。你可通过浏览器或系统选择已从 iCloud Drive 下载的文件，该来源服务由 Apple 按其条款提供。可选 Mac 导入器只读用户选择的已下载文件，不修改原文件，电视应用不要求安装它。

Retention, deletion and backups保留、删除与备份

Book files and covers use caches that tvOS may clear. Reading-state metadata is kept locally until you remove it, restore a backup or uninstall the app, subject to operating system behavior. Removing a local book deletes the app’s copy and associated saved progress, bookmarks and cover, while keeping your external original. Exported backups contain library metadata, settings and reading state, not the EPUB files. Keep your originals and handle backups privately.

原书和封面使用可能被 tvOS 清理的缓存。阅读状态元数据保存在本地，直到你移除、恢复备份或卸载应用，具体仍受系统行为影响。移除本地书籍会删除应用内副本及对应进度、书签、封面，保留外部原文件。导出备份含书库元数据、设置和阅读状态，不含 EPUB；请保留原书并妥善保存备份。

Advertising and analytics广告与分析

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. The website adds no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact-form backend. If you change its language, the site may remember that preference in your browser’s local storage.

应用不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，不跨应用或网站追踪你。网站不添加分析脚本、广告 Cookie 或联系表单后台。切换网站语言时，可将该偏好保存在浏览器本地存储。

Website hosting and support mail网站托管与支持邮件

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests and may process connection information such as IP addresses under its own policies. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message. We use the information you send to answer your request, retain it only as reasonably needed to handle support and applicable legal requirements, and accept deletion requests at the support address. Please do not send private books, passwords or temporary pairing codes.

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。托管方会接收通常的网页请求，并可能按其政策处理 IP 地址等连接信息。发送支持邮件时，你的邮箱服务商及我们的邮箱服务商会处理邮件。我们用你发送的信息回应请求，仅在合理必要的支持处理期间及适用法律要求下保留；可通过支持邮箱提出删除请求。请不要发送私人书籍、密码或临时配对码。

Your choices and contact你的选择与联系

Use the app’s removal and backup controls to manage local reading data, close the upload screen to stop receiving, and disable Home Screen bookshelf in settings if you do not want the artwork shown. There is no developer-operated user account to delete. For support, privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, use the email above.

你可通过应用的移除和备份功能管理本地阅读数据，关闭上传页面停止接收，并在设置关闭主屏幕书架图片。应用没有开发者运营的用户账号可供注销。支持、隐私问题或支持邮件删除请求，请使用上方邮箱。

Changes政策更新

We update this policy when the published product or its data handling changes. The date above identifies the current version. Changes that require permission will follow applicable platform and legal requirements.

当公开产品或数据处理方式变化时会更新本政策，以上日期标明当前版本。需要取得许可的变更将遵循适用的平台及法律要求。
