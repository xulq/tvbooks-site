BookAlong / Privacy随阅 / 隐私政策

Your books stay yours.

你的书，留在你的阅读空间。

Effective / updated: 生效／更新日期：2026-11-04

About this policy关于本政策

BookAlong is published by Johnny Xu. This policy covers this website, local use of the Apple TV and Mac apps, and iCloud library testing in builds where it is enabled. Contact: xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com.

BookAlong（随阅）由 Johnny Xu 发布。本政策适用于本网站、Apple TV 和 Mac 应用的本地使用，以及启用 iCloud 书库的测试版。联系：xiaoqiang.xlq@gmail.com。

Your books and reading data书籍与阅读数据

The apps keep copies of the EPUB and PDF files you choose to add, together with library information, covers, appearance settings, reading positions and bookmarks. Layout and supported in-book search run on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your library or reading history.

应用保存你选择加入的 EPUB 和 PDF 副本，以及书库信息、封面、外观设置、阅读位置和书签。排版和支持的书内搜索在设备上完成。我们不运营接收你书库或阅读历史的服务器。

Local file transfer局域网文件传输

When you open the upload screen on Apple TV, a temporary receiver accepts files and optional reading-state backups from a device you choose on the same network. No pairing code is required. Devices on the same network can use the receiver while it is on. Receiving stops when you manually go back or stop it, the app enters the background, or the ten-minute session expires. Automatic return to the library after upload keeps batch receiving on. This transfer uses local HTTP, so use a network you trust. Files are not routed through our website or a developer-operated cloud service.

在 Apple TV 打开上传页面时，应用临时接收同网络内你选择的设备发送的文件及可选阅读状态备份，无需配对码，接收开启时同网络设备可使用。手动返回/停止、应用进入后台或十分钟会话到期即关闭接收；上传成功自动回书库时继续接收批量文件。局域网传输使用 HTTP，请在可信网络使用。文件不经本网站或开发者云服务中转。

Home Screen artwork主屏幕书架图片

The Home Screen bookshelf is a fixed image included with the app. It does not use your private covers, book titles or reading progress. The app shares only its display switch with its bundled Top Shelf extension through a local App Group. Earlier development preview snapshots and generated private cover walls are removed when the updated app opens.

主屏幕书架为应用内置的固定图片，不使用私人封面、书名或阅读进度。应用通过本地 App Group 仅向自身 Top Shelf 扩展共享显示开关。升级后打开应用会清理此前开发预览版共享的私人书目和墙图。

iCloud library testing and source filesiCloud 书库测试与来源文件

In a build with iCloud library access, enabling sync on Mac uses the private iCloud database for your Apple Account to store book files, covers, library records, reading positions, bookmarks, collections and collection membership. The Apple TV test build is designed to use the same account to browse and download books. Cross-device operation is still being verified. Local reading is available without sync.

在支持 iCloud 书库的版本中，Mac 上启用同步后，原书、封面、书目、阅读位置、书签、集合和书籍所属的集合保存在你的 Apple 账户对应的 iCloud 私有数据库中。Apple TV 测试版使用同一账户浏览和下载书籍的流程，仍待跨设备实机验证。本地阅读无需开启同步。

iCloud is provided by Apple. You can also choose a file downloaded from iCloud Drive for local import. BookAlong keeps its own reading state separately from Apple Books.

iCloud 由 Apple 提供。你也可以选择已从 iCloud Drive 下载的文件进行本地导入。随阅的阅读进度与 Apple Books 独立保存。

Retention, deletion and backups保留、删除与备份

Book files and covers use caches that tvOS may clear. Reading-state metadata is kept locally until you remove it, restore a backup or uninstall the app, subject to operating system behavior. Removing a local book deletes the app’s copy and associated saved progress, bookmarks and cover, while keeping your external original. Exported backups contain library metadata, settings and reading state, without the book files. Keep your originals and handle backups privately.

原书和封面使用可能被 tvOS 清理的缓存。阅读状态元数据保存在本地，直到你移除、恢复备份或卸载应用，具体仍受系统行为影响。移除本地书籍会删除应用内副本及对应进度、书签、封面，保留外部原文件。导出备份含书库元数据、设置和阅读状态，不含原书文件。请保留原书并妥善保存备份。

On Mac, Remove Download clears the downloaded copy from that Mac and retains the synced entry. Remove from Library removes the entry from the synced library and preserves the external source file. You can turn off sync in the app.

在 Mac 上，「移除下载」会清除这台 Mac 上已下载的副本，并保留同步书目。「从书库移除」会从同步书库移除书目，外部原文件仍保留。你可以在应用中关闭同步。

Advertising and analytics广告与分析

The app contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. The website adds no analytics scripts, advertising cookies or contact-form backend. If you change its language, the site may remember that preference in your browser’s local storage.

应用不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，不跨应用或网站追踪你。网站不添加分析脚本、广告 Cookie 或联系表单后台。切换网站语言时，可将该偏好保存在浏览器本地存储。

Website hosting and support mail网站托管与支持邮件

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider receives ordinary web requests and may process connection information such as IP addresses under its own policies. If you email support, your email provider and ours process the message. We use the information you send to answer your request, retain it only as reasonably needed to handle support and applicable legal requirements, and accept deletion requests at the support address. Please do not send private books, passwords or account credentials.

本网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。托管方会接收通常的网页请求，并可能按其政策处理 IP 地址等连接信息。发送支持邮件时，你的邮箱服务商及我们的邮箱服务商会处理邮件。我们用你发送的信息回应请求，仅在合理必要的支持处理期间及适用法律要求下保留；可通过支持邮箱提出删除请求。请不要发送私人书籍、密码或账号凭据。

Your choices and contact你的选择与联系

Use the app’s removal and backup controls to manage local reading data, use Back or Stop on the upload screen to stop receiving, and disable Home Screen bookshelf in settings if you do not want the artwork shown. There is no developer-operated user account to delete. For support, privacy questions or deletion of support correspondence, use the email above.

你可通过应用的移除和备份功能管理本地阅读数据，在上传页面手动返回或停止接收，并在设置关闭主屏幕书架图片。应用没有开发者运营的用户账号可供注销。支持、隐私问题或支持邮件删除请求，请使用上方邮箱。

当公开产品或数据处理方式变化时会更新本政策，以上日期标明当前版本。需要取得许可的变更将遵循适用的平台及法律要求。
