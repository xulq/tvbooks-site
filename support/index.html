Import books on Apple TV在 Apple TV 上导入书籍

  1. On Apple TV, open the library menu and choose Upload local EPUB / PDF.在 Apple TV 书库打开菜单，选择“上传本地 EPUB / PDF”。
  2. Scan the QR code on the television, or open its address in a phone or Mac browser on the same local network.用同一局域网内的手机扫描电视二维码，或用手机/Mac 浏览器打开电视显示的地址。
  3. Choose one or more downloaded EPUB or PDF files. No pairing code is required. Download iCloud Drive files first when necessary.无需配对码，直接选择一份或多份已下载的 EPUB 或 PDF。必要时先下载 iCloud Drive 文件。
  4. After validation, the TV automatically refreshes the library and selects the imported book. Batch uploads continue in the same session.校验完成后，电视自动刷新书库并选中新书，批量上传继续接收后续文件。

Remote controls遥控器操作

  • Right button or right swipe: next page. Left button or left swipe: previous page.右键或右滑：下一页；左键或左滑：上一页。
  • Up/Down: change text size; hold to repeat. Select: reading menu.上／下：调整字号，按住可连续调整；中心键：阅读菜单。
  • Short Play/Pause: contents. Hold Play/Pause: preview landscape/portrait; Select confirms, Back restores.短按播放暂停：目录；长按播放暂停：预览横竖屏，中心确认、返回恢复。
  • In the library, directional buttons choose a book, Select opens it, and holding Select opens book options.书库中方向键选书、中心打开、长按中心打开图书选项。

Open and organize books on Mac在 Mac 上打开和整理书籍

In the Mac test build, choose File → Open Book, open an EPUB or PDF with BookAlong from Finder, or drag the file into the app. The book opens and is copied into your library. Double-click a book to read it, press Return on a selected book to rename it, and use collections to organize your library.

在 Mac 测试版中，选择「文件 → 打开书籍」，从 Finder 用随阅打开 EPUB 或 PDF，或把文件拖进应用。书籍打开时会复制一份到书库。双击书籍可阅读，选中书籍后按 Return 可改名，也可以用集合整理书籍。

The local Mac build was tested on Apple silicon and requires macOS 14 or later. See the current platform availability.

Mac 本地版已在 Apple 芯片设备上验证，要求 macOS 14 或更高版本。可查看各端进度。

Common questions常见问题

Can every chapter fit on one screen?所有章节都能一屏读完吗？

No single layout fits every chapter. A short chapter can fit with suitable text and spacing; long chapters still paginate. Landscape supports one, two or three columns, with two as the default.

每屏容纳量取决于章节长度、字号、行距和方向。合适的短章节可一屏呈现，长章节仍正常分页。横屏支持一、二、三栏，默认两栏。

What does portrait mode require?竖屏模式需要什么？

A monitor you can physically rotate. BookAlong compensates the page for a 90° or 270° display rotation. tvOS system screens remain in the system orientation.

需要能够物理旋转的显示器。BookAlong 为显示器的 90° 或 270° 旋转补偿页面方向；tvOS 系统页面仍保持系统方向。

Which book formats are supported?支持哪些书籍格式？

BookAlong reads DRM-free reflowable EPUB 2/3, PDF and compatible image-based EPUB comics, including KCC exports. The limit is 80 MiB per file. Complex fixed-layout EPUB and vertical writing are outside the supported formats.

支持无 DRM 的可重排 EPUB 2/3、PDF 和兼容的图片型 EPUB 漫画，包括 KCC 导出的漫画。单文件上限为 80 MiB。复杂固定版式 EPUB 和竖排文字暂不支持。

Why has a downloaded book become unavailable?为什么已导入的书提示需要恢复原书？

tvOS can evict cached book files. Keep the originals and export a reading-state backup. Uploading the exact same source file restores the book cache while preserving saved progress and bookmarks.

tvOS 可能清理原书缓存。请保留源文件并导出阅读状态备份；重新上传完全相同的原文件可恢复缓存并保留已保存的进度和书签。

Does BookAlong synchronize Apple Books progress?会同步 Apple Books 的阅读进度吗？

No. BookAlong maintains its own reading position. It does not access Apple Books’ private cloud service, import DRM purchases or synchronize its private progress format.

不会。BookAlong 独立保存阅读位置，不访问 Apple Books 私有云服务、不导入 DRM 购书，也不持续同步其私有进度格式。

Is iCloud required?必须使用 iCloud 吗？

Local upload and reading work without iCloud. You can select downloaded iCloud Drive files in your browser’s file picker. The separate iCloud library is being tested, with Mac-to-Apple TV reading continuity still to be verified.

本地上传和阅读无需 iCloud。浏览器可选择已下载的 iCloud Drive 文件。随阅的 iCloud 书库正在测试，Mac 与 Apple TV 的接续阅读仍待验证。

Is BookAlong an Apple app?BookAlong 是 Apple 官方应用吗？

BookAlong is independently developed. Apple TV and Apple Books are Apple products. BookAlong takes inspiration from a calm reading experience and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.

BookAlong 为独立开发产品。Apple TV 和 Apple Books 为 Apple 产品；BookAlong 以简洁阅读体验为目标，与 Apple 无隶属或背书关系。
