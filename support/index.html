Import without a Mac companion无需 Mac 工具，本地导入

  1. On Apple TV, switch to Local library and open Upload.在 Apple TV 切到「本地书库」，打开「上传」。
  2. Connect your phone or computer to the same trusted network. Scan the QR code or open the URL displayed on the TV.手机或电脑连接同一可信网络，扫描电视二维码或打开电视显示的网址。
  3. Choose a downloaded DRM-free EPUB or PDF. Wait for the success message; the library refreshes automatically.选择已下载、无 DRM 的 EPUB 或 PDF；上传成功后书库自动刷新。

No pairing code is required. The local receiver uses HTTP and stays open for batch uploads until you stop it, go back manually, leave the app or reach the ten-minute limit. Use a trusted private network. A guest network or client isolation can prevent devices from connecting.

无需配对码。局域网接收使用 HTTP；批量上传期间持续开启，直到手动停止或返回、应用退到后台，或十分钟会话到期。请使用可信私人网络。访客网络或设备隔离可能阻止连接。

Read with the remote用遥控器阅读

  • Left / Right or a swipe: turn pages. Up / Down: change EPUB text size.左右方向键或左右滑动：翻页。上下方向键：调整 EPUB 字号。
  • Select: reading menu. Play / Pause: contents while reading; library menu on the shelf.中心键：阅读菜单。播放／暂停：阅读时打开目录，书库中打开书库菜单。
  • Hold Select while reading: open the current page’s illustrations. PDF and comic controls are shown in their reading menus.阅读时长按中心键：查看本页图片。PDF 和漫画的操作见各自阅读菜单。
  • Hold Play / Pause: preview landscape or your last portrait direction. Confirm to keep; otherwise it reverts. Portrait requires physically rotating a suitable display.长按播放／暂停：预览横屏与上次竖屏方向，确认后保留，否则自动恢复。竖屏需物理旋转兼容显示器。

Mac: Books → BookAlongMac：Books → 随阅

The current Mac app is a menu-bar companion, not the older Mac library and reader. It requires macOS 14 or later and Apple silicon. It is being tested for distribution outside the Mac App Store; there is no public download yet.

当前 Mac 应用是菜单栏后台同步工具，已替代旧版 Mac 书库与阅读器。要求 macOS 14 起及 Apple 芯片，正在测试 Mac App Store 外分发，尚无公开下载。

  1. Install the signed test app in Applications and open its menu-bar item. Choose Enable Sync.将签名测试版放入「应用程序」，打开菜单栏入口，选择「开启同步」。
  2. Allow the system request to read Books data. If access is still denied, grant this installed app Full Disk Access in System Settings → Privacy & Security, then quit and reopen it. This is a broad system permission; enable it only if you want automatic Books mirroring.允许系统读取 Books 数据的提示。若仍无法读取，在「系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 完全磁盘访问权限」中授权这个已安装的应用，然后退出重开。这是系统提供的较广权限，仅在希望自动同步 Books 时启用。
  3. Use the same iCloud Apple Account on Mac and Apple TV. On the TV select iCloud library and Refresh library. Both apps must use the same production container.Mac 与 Apple TV 使用同一 iCloud Apple 账户。电视端选择「iCloud 书库」并刷新；两端需使用同一生产容器。
  4. Choose all books or the first N supported books, and your preferred overflow behavior. Follow Books order or select a matching sort mode. Launch at login is optional.选择全部或前 N 本支持的书籍，设置超出范围时的处理方式；排序可跟随 Books，或选择与 Books 相同的模式。登录时启动由你选择。

The companion reads book metadata and supported files. It does not read, sync or write back Apple Books reading positions, annotations or bookmarks. Books purchases protected by DRM are skipped. This app uses its own private iCloud library; it is not direct access to an Apple Books cloud library.工具读取书目与支持的原书，不读取、同步或写回 Apple Books 阅读进度、批注和书签。DRM 购书会跳过。随阅使用独立私有 iCloud 书库，不是直接访问 Apple Books 云书库。

Removing a mirrored source book from Books can remove its mirrored BookAlong entry after confirmation. A failed or incomplete source scan does not trigger removal. Pausing sync stops future batches; it does not erase previous uploads. Changing the selected range can also affect mirrored entries according to the overflow option you choose.

从 Books 书库移除来源书籍，确认后会移除对应的随阅镜像书目；扫描失败或不完整时不推断删除。暂停同步停止后续批次，不会擦除已上传内容。改变同步范围也可能按你选择的超范围策略影响镜像书目。

The matching TV update is needed for Books order. End-to-end TV checks remain pending. The Mac app does not need Accessibility, Screen Recording or automation control of Books.

Books 顺序需要配套电视更新，完整电视端实机验证仍待完成。Mac 工具不需要辅助功能、屏幕录制或自动化控制 Books 的权限。

Supported files and limits支持范围与限制

DRM-free reflowable EPUB 2/3, PDFs, and compatible image-based fixed-layout EPUB comics. Up to 80 MiB per book and 2,000 cloud entries. Complex fixed layouts, vertical writing, complex standalone SVG and formulas may not render faithfully. Scanned PDFs have no searchable text unless the file already contains it.

支持无 DRM 的可重排 EPUB 2/3、PDF，以及兼容的图片型固定版式 EPUB 漫画。单本上限 80 MiB，云书目上限 2,000 本。复杂固定版式、竖排文字、复杂独立 SVG 和公式可能无法完整呈现。扫描 PDF 若无文字层，不能进行文字搜索。

Keep an original copy保留原书与备份

tvOS may clear cached book files and covers. Keep original files elsewhere. Exported reading-state backups include metadata, preferences, progress and bookmarks, not book files. Downloading a synced book again can restore its local copy. Back up before testing source removals or switching accounts.

tvOS 可能清理原书与封面缓存，请在应用外保留原文件。导出的阅读状态备份包含书目、偏好、进度与书签，不含原书。云书籍可重新下载恢复本地副本。测试来源删除或切换账号前先备份。

Common questions常见问题

Does the TV need my Mac to stay on?阅读时 Mac 要一直开着吗？

No. Once a book is downloaded, the TV reads it locally. New Books changes require the Mac companion to run and sync.

不需要。书籍下载后在电视本地阅读；Books 的新增和变化需要 Mac 工具运行并完成同步。

Where is my Apple Books reading position?为什么没有 Apple Books 阅读进度？

Reading positions are separate. The current companion mirrors book files and order, not Apple Books progress.

两者进度独立。当前工具同步原书和顺序，不同步 Apple Books 阅读位置。

Why is a book missing?为什么有些书没有同步？

Check the companion’s unsynced items. The source may be protected, unsupported, too large or not downloaded yet. Wait for Books to download it.

查看工具中的未同步项。来源可能受 DRM 保护、格式不支持、超过大小限制，或尚未下载；请先在 Books 下载。

Can I buy it now?现在可以购买吗？

Apple TV is in TestFlight testing. A public release and the Mac download have not been announced. iPhone and iPad are planned.

Apple TV 正在 TestFlight 测试，正式版与 Mac 公开下载尚未发布。iPhone 和 iPad 仍在规划中。
