01 / The reading room大屏阅读

Read at your
own distance.远一点，近一点。
都读得自在。

Sit back on the sofa or stand up for a change. Press Left or Right, or swipe, to turn a page. Adjust the text size with the remote to suit where you are.

坐着读，或站起来接着读。轻按左右方向键，或左右滑动，就能翻页。用遥控器调整字号，找到适合观看距离的文字大小。

Choose one, two or three columns for EPUB text. With a short chapter and the right type size, more of the thought can stay in view. Longer chapters turn into pages naturally.

EPUB 正文可选一栏、两栏或三栏。长度合适的短章节，用适合的字号可以一屏读完，前后的内容更容易对照；较长章节仍会自然分页。

01

Make the page yours.把页面调成喜欢的样子。

Text size, line spacing, margins and themes. Reflow keeps your reading position; page turns leave the text undisturbed.

字号、行距、页边距与主题随你调整。重排保留阅读位置，翻页时不弹出操作提示。

02

Look a little closer.图文，都能细看。

Read PDFs in their original layout. Zoom and move around pages, explore compatible image-based EPUB comics, or open a book illustration full screen.

PDF 保留原版布局，可缩放和移动；兼容的图片型 EPUB 漫画支持多页阅读，正文插图也能打开全屏细看。

03

A different way to hold a book.换个方向，继续读。

Use landscape, or physically rotate a compatible display for portrait reading. Preview a direction before keeping it. Portrait requires a rotatable display.

横屏展开阅读，也可物理旋转兼容的显示器，读成一本大号的书。方向切换先预览再确认；竖屏需要可旋转的显示器。

02 / Bring your own books带上自己的书

A book. A remote.
You’re ready.一本书，一个遥控器。
开始读吧。

Apple TV works on its own. No Mac companion or BookAlong account is required for local reading. An original welcome book is included so you can try the controls straight away.

Apple TV 可以独立使用。本地阅读无需 Mac 同步工具，也不必注册随阅账号。内置原创入门书，打开就能试试阅读与遥控器操作。

03 / Mac companionMac 后台同步

Keep Books on your Mac.
Bring the shelf to your TV.Books 留在 Mac。
书架，来到电视。

A small menu-bar companion mirrors supported books from Apple Books into your private BookAlong iCloud library. Keep using Books on your Mac; use BookAlong for the big screen.

一个轻巧的菜单栏工具，把 Apple Books 中支持的书籍同步到随阅私有 iCloud 书库。Mac 上继续使用 Books，大屏上交给随阅。

How the companion works了解同步与权限

Books, order and changes书籍、顺序与变化

Sync supported DRM-free EPUB and PDF files and the All Books order. Choose all supported books or the first N. Source removals are confirmed before mirrored entries are removed.

同步无 DRM EPUB、PDF 与「全部书籍」顺序，可选择全部或前 N 本。来源移除经过确认后，才移除对应的镜像书目。

You control the background后台运行，由你掌握

Enable or pause sync, check unsynced items, and choose whether to launch at login. The companion needs permission to read Books data.

自主开启、暂停同步，查看未同步项，并选择是否登录时启动。工具需要你授权读取 Books 数据。

macOS 14+ · Apple silicon · Testing, distributed outside the Mac App Store. Does not sync or write back Apple Books reading positions. The matching TV update and cross-device checks are still pending.macOS 14 起 · Apple 芯片 · 测试中，采用 Mac App Store 外分发。不同步或写回 Apple Books 阅读进度。配套电视更新与跨设备验证仍待完成。

04 / Your private shelf自己的私人书库

Your books.
Your quiet space.自己的书。
安静地读。

No advertising, no third-party analytics, no developer account. Local files stay on your TV; optional cloud books use the private iCloud database for your Apple Account.

没有广告、第三方分析或开发者账号。本地书籍保存在电视上；可选云书库使用你 Apple 账户对应的 iCloud 私有数据库。

BookAlong does not unlock DRM-protected purchases. Keep your original files: tvOS can clear downloaded caches. Up to 80 MiB per book and 2,000 cloud entries.

随阅不解锁 DRM 购书。请保留原文件：tvOS 可能清理已下载的缓存。单本上限 80 MiB，云书目上限 2,000 本。
